\# AI or Not: Question Classification



This document classifies university service questions into questions that need AI and questions that can be answered without AI.



\## 1. Questions That Need AI



| No. | Question | Reason |

|---|---|---|

| 1 | Which university office should I contact for my situation? | AI can interpret the user's situation and recommend a relevant office. |

| 2 | Can you explain this university regulation in simple words? | AI can simplify complex text into natural language. |

| 3 | Can you summarize this long university document? | AI can extract and summarize the main points. |

| 4 | What is the difference between these two university policies? | AI can compare policies and explain their differences. |

| 5 | I am not sure which administrative procedure applies to me. Can you help? | AI can interpret the user's context and identify a likely procedure. |

| 6 | Can you help me write an email to a university office? | AI can generate an email based on the user's needs. |

| 7 | How should I explain my complicated academic problem to the university? | AI can organize the user's description into a clear explanation. |

| 8 | Which university instructions are relevant to my situation? | AI can interpret the context and identify relevant instructions. |

| 9 | What steps could I take to resolve my university service issue? | AI can explain possible steps based on the user's context. |

| 10 | Can you explain this administrative requirement with an example? | AI can generate a contextual explanation and example. |



\## 2. Questions That Do Not Need AI



| No. | Question | Reason |

|---|---|---|

| 1 | Where is the Training Office located? | A predefined office directory can provide the location. |

| 2 | What are the opening hours of the Student Affairs Office? | Stored office hours can be retrieved directly. |

| 3 | What is the university's official email address? | The contact directory contains this information. |

| 4 | What is the phone number of the library? | A direct lookup can return the phone number. |

| 5 | Where can I find the university academic calendar? | The system can return a stored official link. |

| 6 | What documents are required to replace a student ID? | A predefined checklist can provide the required documents. |

| 7 | What is the tuition payment deadline? | A current, stored deadline can be retrieved directly. |

| 8 | Where can I download the student application form? | A direct link to the official form is sufficient. |

| 9 | What time does the library open? | The library schedule can provide the answer. |

| 10 | How can I contact the IT support team? | Stored contact information can be returned directly. |



\## 3. Conclusion



AI is useful for questions requiring contextual understanding, document summarization, policy comparison, or natural-language generation.



AI is generally unnecessary for simple factual questions that can be answered accurately using predefined data, such as office locations, contact details, opening hours, deadlines, and official links.



The system should prefer direct lookup for simple factual questions and use AI when contextual interpretation or natural-language generation is needed. Official university information should remain the source of truth.
